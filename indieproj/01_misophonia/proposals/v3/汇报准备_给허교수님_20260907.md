## 🎤 口头汇报脚本

> **Professor Heo, hello. This is the v3 update for the misophonia project. Three key changes:**
>
> **First, I've redefined appraisal from "anxiety sensitivity" to "threat-related interpretation of bodily sensations" — a transdiagnostic tendency to evaluate interoceptive signals as dangerous, intolerable, or violating. ASI-3 Physical Concerns is just one operational index of this broader construct. This aligns with Rosenthal et al. 2026 (ARCP) showing misophonia's dominant affect is anger/irritation, not fear/anxiety.**
>
> **Second, Layer 2 is now positioned as the "Bridge Layer" — 3,360 trial-level observations testing whether trait-level threat interpretation specifically amplifies the *immediate affective transformation* of trigger sounds into anger/irritation. This bridges trait-level constructs (Layer 1) and neural mechanisms (Layer 3).**
>
> **Third, Layer 3 is reframed as "Convergent Neural Correlates" — we drop the rigid anterior=appraisal/posterior=registration mapping. Instead, we test whether interoceptive processing (Heart+Gut>Visual) and salience network organization *converge* on the trait→trigger pathway established in Layers 1–2.**
>
> **Four things I need your input on today: (1) Can we get behavioral log samples this week? (2) Confirm Layer 3 preregistration-only won't conflict with lab's existing interoceptive fMRI work. (3) Your take on what neural interpretation the Heart/Gut/Visual interoceptive attention task best supports — I don't want to pre-assign anterior=appraisal. (4) IRB pathway for secondary analysis: 연구자 추가 vs 변경 보고. Ten minutes before lab meeting should suffice.**
